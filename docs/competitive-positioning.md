# Product Positioning Boundaries

**Product:** Ontario Economic & Business Intelligence Platform
**Document Classification:** Product positioning (implementation-aligned)
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## What the current product provides

- Ontario municipal, demographic, business-count, municipal-finance, and opportunity-analysis views backed by the platform's stored datasets.
- Endpoint-level provenance and freshness metadata where exposed by the API.
- A read-only location-feasibility dossier preview that a user can print or save locally.

## What the current product does not provide

- Checkout and paid distribution. Dossier checkout currently returns `503 Service Unavailable`; the preview does not certify lending decisions or support CSBFP applications.
- Public alert-event browsing, alert evaluation, notification-queue access, or operational alert delivery. The retained public alert endpoint records registration only.
- Comparative pricing, coverage, accuracy, or capability claims about named competitors. Such statements require current primary-source evidence before publication.

## Evidence standard for future positioning

Any comparative claim must identify a dated primary source, the exact product or plan compared, and the measurement or definition used. Any commercial claim must match an implemented, user-accessible workflow and its terms.
