# T-006 — Build event/diff change detection ledger for listings, prices, and dataset releases

**Source finding:** User Amendment #6 | Severity: P1 | Class: MISSING  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 4h | **Sequence:** after T-003

## WHAT
Implement an event/diff change-detection engine that inspects newly ingested or updated records against the persistent database to detect: new commercial business listings, asking price drops/increases, relisting events, commercial rent benchmark changes, new municipal budget filings, and StatCan dataset revisions. Detected changes are persisted to a structured `audit_events` ledger with metadata. Decouple change detection from notification delivery by creating a subscriber watch evaluation service capable of matching watches (e.g. category, geography, radius, indicator change thresholds) against the event ledger.

## WHY
Automated change alerts provide a strong recurring value proposition for commercial real estate brokers, franchisees, and municipal planners. Decoupling detection from delivery prevents polling upstream providers multiple times per subscriber.
