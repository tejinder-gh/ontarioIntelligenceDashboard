# T-005 — Implement streaming census bulk ingestion pipeline and architectural benchmark

**Source finding:** User Amendment #1 | Severity: P0 | Class: BROKEN  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 6h | **Sequence:** after T-003

## WHAT
Discover and connect the official current Statistics Canada 2021 Census of Population Profile bulk data source for Ontario (replacing outdated legacy references). Build a high-performance streaming ingestion pipeline that parses Census Subdivisions (CSDs), normalizes key indicators (population, age, households, median/average income, shelter costs, workforce participation), and persists them with full provenance. Benchmark the end-to-end pipeline measuring: compressed download size, uncompressed size, rows processed, peak RAM, ingestion duration, database size, index-build duration, city-query latency, and province-wide ranking latency.

## WHY
Moving from 8 hardcoded municipalities to the complete Ontario dataset is the single biggest requirement to eliminate the "436-city ghost town" defect. Benchmarking validates that streaming ingestion and local querying deliver sub-50ms latency without RAM exhaustion.
