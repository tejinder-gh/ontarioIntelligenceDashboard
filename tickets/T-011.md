# T-011 — Ingestion Expansion: Authentic Census 2021 Profiles for All 444 Ontario Municipalities

**Source finding:** User Spec §6, §8, §43, §44 | Severity: P1 | Class: DATA_INGESTION  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 6h | **Sequence:** P1

## WHAT
Expand the Census 2021 ingestion adapter from the 8-city hardcoded stub to ingest complete 2021 Census profiles across all 444 Ontario municipalities.
Ingest and normalize:
- Total population, 2016 population, 5-year growth %, land area, population density
- Age groups: 0–14, 15–19, 20–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55–64, 65–74, 75+ (both counts AND percentage shares)
- Household structure: average household size, couple with children, couple without children, lone parent, one-person households
- Housing stock: detached, semi-detached, townhouse/row, apartment (high-rise / low-rise)
- Total household income: median, average, after-tax median, low income rate
- Labour indicators: participation rate, employment rate, unemployment rate
- Commuting mode: public transit, car driver, active commute

## WHY
Resolves the "436-city ghost town" problem. Every Ontario municipality must have authoritative demographic baseline data instead of empty or pending states.
