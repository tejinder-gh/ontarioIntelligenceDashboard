# T-008 — Build 1-click lender-ready Location Feasibility Dossier preview and PDF foundation

**Source finding:** User Amendment #8, §CFO-1 | Severity: P1 | Class: MISSING  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 5h | **Sequence:** after T-001, T-002

## WHAT
Build a dedicated "Feasibility Dossier" generator and preview modal that synthesizes a municipality's and business category's complete economic profile into a structured, lender-ready feasibility report. For Burlington (and other seeded municipalities), the dossier integrates: executive summary, population & 5-year growth trajectory, household income distribution (mean vs median), top NOC occupations & industries, verified employer business counts by employee band (Table 33-10-1097-01), commercial real estate rent baselines, category saturation gap index, verifiable franchise royalty revenue chains (COGS, labor, rent, SDE), and competitive footprint map. Provide a clean printable/PDF view with simulated paywall/lead capture triggers to validate willingness to pay.

## WHY
Entrepreneurs and franchisees need concrete, professional documentation to secure commercial bank financing (Canada Small Business Financing Program - CSBFP) and commercial lease approval from institutional landlords. This report represents our primary $199 CAD pay-per-report monetization vehicle.
