# T-001 — Replace hardcoded Burlington population in per-capita calculations

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §CTO-2 | Severity: P0 | Class: BROKEN  
**Assigned tier:** INTERMEDIATE  
**Effort estimate:** 2h | **Sequence:** independent

## WHAT
In `CompetitionView.tsx`, `MunicipalityFinancesView.tsx`, and `opportunity-engine.ts`, per-capita and saturation calculations currently divide by a hardcoded `186948` (Burlington's population). This ticket replaces all hardcoded population instances with the active city's dynamic population retrieved from the profile or observation data, with graceful non-zero fallbacks that do not distort calculations for other municipalities.

## WHY
When viewing Toronto, Ottawa, Milton, or any non-Burlington city, per-capita metrics (e.g. municipal budget per resident, business saturation per 10k residents) are severely distorted by dividing by Burlington's population rather than the active city's actual population.
