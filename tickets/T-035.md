# T-035 — Embed Feasibility Dossier triggers in Opportunity Lab & Business Listings

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §CFO-3 / CPO-3 | Severity: P1 | Class: MISSING  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** 3

## WHAT
Connect the $199 Location Feasibility Dossier modal directly to the primary discovery views:
1. In `src/client/views/OpportunityLabView.tsx`, add an "Export Banker Dossier ($199)" action button on each opportunity recommendation card that opens `FeasibilityDossierModal` pre-populated with the target municipality and category.
2. In `src/client/views/BusinessListingsView.tsx`, add an "Inspect Location Dossier ($199)" button on commercial listing cards that opens `FeasibilityDossierModal` pre-populated with the listing's city and category.

## WHY
Stops funnel leakage where prospective franchise buyers discover high-fit opportunities but have no intuitive path to purchase lender-ready due diligence.

- `src/client/views/BusinessListingsView.tsx`
