# T-012 — Population Estimates (POP-CSD-EST Table 17-10-0155-01) & Projections (POP-CSD-PROJ)

**Source finding:** User Spec §6, §8 | Severity: P1 | Class: DATA_INGESTION  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** P1

## WHAT
Implement the adapter for StatCan Table 17-10-0155-01 (Population estimates, July 1, by Census Subdivision, 2021 boundaries) and population projections (Table 17-10-0162-01).
- Keep Census counts (May 2021) and July 1 annual estimates strictly separated with clear reference periods.
- Record latest annual estimates, annual growth rate, and recent trajectory.
- Ensure projections are classified as `MODELED` and visually indicated as scenario models.

## WHY
Census data is quinquennial (2021). Entrepreneurs and investors need recent annual population estimates (e.g. 2024/2025) and scenario projections to evaluate current market size and growth momentum.
