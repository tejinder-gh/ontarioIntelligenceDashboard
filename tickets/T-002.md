# T-002 — Remove deceptive fallbacks and correct coverage reporting

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §CTO-1, §CTO-3 | Severity: P0 | Class: BROKEN  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** independent

## WHAT
In `OverviewView.tsx`, unobserved metrics silently fall back to Burlington's exact numbers (income $116k, 5,820 businesses, rent $34.50, unemployment 6.6%). Simultaneously, in `src/server/routes.ts`, requesting a profile for a city without coverage returns a hallucinated `95.0%` coverage report with `HIGH` confidence. This ticket removes the synthetic Burlington fallback numbers in `OverviewView.tsx`, replaces them with explicit `null`/unobserved handling with informative empty-state indicators, and ensures `routes.ts` reports truthful coverage metrics (0% coverage, `LOW` confidence) when observations are absent.

## WHY
Silently presenting Burlington's affluent economic data for unseeded municipalities (like Sudbury, Kenora, or Timmins) and claiming 95% data coverage directly violates the platform's core commitment to empirical, zero-guesswork intelligence.
