# T-004 — Fix Opportunity Workflow B competitor calculation and population filtering

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §CTO-4, §CPO-Journey-3 | Severity: P0 | Class: BROKEN  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** after T-001

## WHAT
In `src/analytics/opportunity-engine.ts`, `runWorkflowB` relies on counting rows in the `businesses` table to determine existing competitors. Because `businesses` only contains 21 OSM records across 3 cities, `runWorkflowB` reports 0 existing competitors for major cities like Toronto and Ottawa, generating artificially inflated opportunity scores. This ticket updates `runWorkflowB` to check for official employer business counts from Table 33-10-1097 / observations when OSM location counts are absent or sparse. Additionally, it updates `runWorkflowA` to accept a configurable `minPopulation` filter (defaulting to 0 rather than a rigid `> 50000`) so entrepreneurs researching small and mid-sized Ontario municipalities are not excluded.

## WHY
False gap scores in major cities destroy user confidence, and hiding municipalities under 50,000 population shuts out 85% of Ontario communities from feasibility analysis.
