# T-013 — Retail Fuel Pricing & Gas Price Delta Engine (FUEL-RETAIL Table 18-10-0001-01)

**Source finding:** User Spec §6, §11 | Severity: P1 | Class: ANALYTICS & INGESTION  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** P1

## WHAT
Implement ingestion and comparative delta analytics for retail gasoline and fuel prices across Ontario using StatCan Table 18-10-0001-01.
For any selected municipality:
- Show actual latest observed gas price (cents/L or $/L)
- Show Toronto reference price for the identical product and reference month
- Compute absolute price delta (e.g. Burlington $1.49/L vs Toronto $1.55/L -> -$0.06/L)
- Compute percentage price delta (e.g. -3.9%)
- Retain historical monthly price trend

## WHY
Fuel and transportation operating costs directly impact logistics, commuter patterns, retail basket sizes, and commercial service margins.
